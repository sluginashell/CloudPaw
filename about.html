About CloudPaw

Connecting shelters and rescues with people ready to adopt.

Our Mission

Every year, millions of pets wait in shelters for a home. CloudPaw makes it easier for adopters to find them by bringing listings from shelters and rescues into one simple, searchable place.

We believe adopting should be quick, transparent, and stress-free for families and for the shelters doing the hard work.

For Shelters

Shelters and rescues can list pets for free, keep listings up to date, and receive adoption applications in one place. Get in touch below to set up an account.
